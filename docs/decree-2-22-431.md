# Moroccan Public Procurement Decree n° 2-22-431 — Technical Reference

**Décret n° 2-22-431 du 15 chaabane 1444 (8 mars 2023) relatif aux marchés publics**  
*Entered into force on September 1st, 2023.*

This reference details the mathematical formulas, threshold corridors, and procedural rules implemented by Soumit's compliance engine and MCP server.

---

## 1. Financial Verification Corridors (Articles 44 & 45)

When public opening commissions evaluate bids, financial offers are evaluated against the buyer's confidential administrative estimate (*l'estimation du maître d'ouvrage*, established prior to opening under Article 5).

### Article 44: Offre Excessive (Excessive Bid)

An offer is deemed excessive and is **eliminated automatically** (*écartée d'office*) if it exceeds the administrative estimate by more than **20%**, regardless of sector:

$$\text{Plafond Offre Excessive} = \text{Estimation}_{\text{HT}} \times 1.20$$

If $\text{Offre}_{\text{HT}} > \text{Plafond}$, the bidder is rejected with no recourse.

### Article 44 & 45: Offre Anormalement Basse (Abnormally Low Bid)

An offer is suspected of being abnormally low if it falls below the administrative estimate by more than the statutory discount ceiling:

| Nature de la Prestation | Seuil Légal de Rabais | Plancher Offre Anormalement Basse |
|---|---|---|
| **Travaux** (Construction, BTP, Génie civil) | **-20%** | $\text{Estimation}_{\text{HT}} \times 0.80$ |
| **Fournitures & Services** (Médical, IT, Conseil, Maintenance) | **-25%** | $\text{Estimation}_{\text{HT}} \times 0.75$ |

#### Commission Procedure for Abnormally Low Bids:
1. If $\text{Offre}_{\text{HT}} < \text{Plancher}$, the bid is not immediately discarded.
2. Under Article 45, the commission must send a formal request for detailed price breakdowns (*sous-détails des prix*) or technical justifications.
3. The bidder is granted a strict window (typically 7 to 10 days) to prove the economic viability of their price.
4. Failure to satisfy the commission results in rejection.

## 2. Mandatory Display Deadlines (Article 23)

Under Article 23, public consultation notices must be published on the portal `marchespublics.gov.ma` and in at least two national newspapers (one in Arabic, one in a foreign language) for a minimum mandatory duration prior to the bid opening session (*séance d'ouverture des plis*):

| Category | Threshold Condition | Minimum Display Period |
|---|---|---|
| **Standard Procedures** | Budget $\le$ 10,000,000 MAD HT (Travaux)<br>Budget $\le$ 1,600,000 MAD HT (Fournitures/Services) | **21 jours francs minimum** |
| **Major Consultations (Marchés d'envergure)** | Budget $>$ 10,000,000 MAD HT (Travaux)<br>Budget $>$ 1,600,000 MAD HT (Fournitures/Services) | **40 jours francs minimum** |
| **Urgent Procedures** | Declared urgent with written justification by Master of Work | **15 jours francs minimum** |

*Note: Soumit automatically verifies notice timestamp deltas against these statutory durations to flag procedural vulnerability.*

## 3. Administrative Folder Compliance (Articles 27 & 28)

At the first public opening session, the commission opens the administrative dossier (*dossier administratif*). Any non-compliance here leads to **immediate rejection without evaluation of technical or financial offers**:

### Critical Eliminatory Checks:
1. **Cautionnement Provisoire (Provisional Guarantee / Bid Bond)**:
   - Must be presented in original bank guarantee or receipt from CDG (*Caisse de Dépôt et de Gestion*).
   - Must strictly equal the exact MAD amount specified in Article 20 of the Règlement de Consultation.
   - Must remain valid for at least **30 days beyond the bid validity period** (typically 90 + 30 = 120 days).
2. **Déclaration sur l'honneur (Declaration on Honor)**:
   - Must conform to the official statutory template under Article 27.
   - Must certify fiscal regularity, CNSS registration, legal non-bankruptcy, and absence of exclusion.
3. **Visite des Lieux (Site Visit Certificate)**:
   - When mandated by the Règlement de Consultation, the certificate signed by the buyer is mandatory. Absence is eliminatory.

---

## 4. Bon de Commande Ceiling (Article 148)

For simplified purchasing orders (*Bons de commande*):
- Standard annual ceiling per budget line: **500,000 MAD TTC**.
- Exceptions (specific public establishments): May be elevated to **1,000,000 MAD TTC** by express ministerial decree.
- Mandatory competitive quotation: Minimum of **3 concurrent written quotations** required before placement.

```typescript
import { computeDecreeCompliance } from "@vext-ma/soumit-mcp";

const audit = computeDecreeCompliance({
  estimatedAmountHt: 4_000_000,
  proposedAmountHt: 2_900_000,
  sectorType: "fournitures", // -25% ceiling = 3,000,000 MAD
});

console.log(audit.status); // "OFFRE_ANORMALEMENT_BASSE"
console.log(audit.floorAmount); // 3000000
```
